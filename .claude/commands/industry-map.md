Read `03-gemini-report.md` and `04-critique.md`. Use only claims the critique
tagged `hard` or `soft`; ignore `unsupported` ones.

Apply the value-capture discipline from CLAUDE.md. Write `05-industry-map.md`:

1. **Layer map** — decompose the industry into its layers (the "cake"). For
   each layer, a row with: what it does, representative players (public/
   private), margin structure, who holds pricing power, and **investable?**
   (public, liquid, and a meaningful needle-mover — yes/no).
2. **Where the profit pools** — name the 1–2 layers that capture durable
   economics and say *why it stays there* (what stops it commoditising).
   Call out any mismatch between where the excitement is and where the money is.
3. **Where the subject company sits** — which layer(s), and whether that is a
   profit-pooling layer or not.
4. **Chosen layer + technology to deep-dive** — pick the layer for stage 6 and
   the specific technology/sub-segment within it, with the one-line substitution
   thesis (new X replaces old Y).
5. **Open questions.**

Distinguish your inferences from the report's claims throughout.
