Read `04c-primary-financials.md` and (if run) `03-gemini-report.md` +
`04-critique.md`, using only claims tagged `hard`/`soft`; ignore `unsupported` ones.

This is the **company-funnel** industry step — it exists to position ONE named
issuer, not to survey the whole industry. Keep it lean: enough of the value chain
to see who competes with the subject, where the profit pool sits relative to it,
and whether that pool is defensible. **A full layer-by-layer survey with
investability ratings for every layer is the industry track's job** (`/industry-
layermap`) — do not reproduce it here.

Apply the value-capture discipline from CLAUDE.md. Write `05-industry-map.md`:

1. **The subject's slice of the value chain.** A short map of only the layers that
   touch the subject: the layer(s) it occupies, the layer directly above (its
   customer / who can disintermediate it) and below (its supplier / who it is a
   price-taker to). For each, one line on margin structure and who holds pricing
   power. Skip layers the subject doesn't touch.
2. **Where the profit pool sits vs the subject.** Name the 1–2 layers that capture
   durable economics and say *why it stays there* (what stops it commoditising) —
   and whether the subject is IN that pool, above it, or below it. Call out any
   mismatch between where the excitement is and where the money is.
3. **The competitor set — named, on the subject's own primitive.** List the direct
   competitors (public and private) and, where available, their size on the SAME
   primitive the subject is measured on (e.g. competitor TPV). This seeds the
   competitor benchmarking in `/operating-drivers` — flag it as a `hard` gap if the
   peer volumes aren't sourced yet; it is mandatory downstream, not optional.
4. **The layer to deep-dive.** Pick the layer/sub-segment `/operating-drivers` should
   build the substitution + volume×price model around (usually the subject's
   fastest-growing or most-contested layer), with the one-line substitution thesis
   (new X replaces old Y).

Distinguish your inferences from the report's claims throughout. Append unresolved
items to the run's disclosure-gap register (see CLAUDE.md).
