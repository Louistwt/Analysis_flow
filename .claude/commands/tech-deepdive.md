Read `05-industry-map.md` (its chosen layer + technology) and the operating
drivers from `01-classification.md`.

Scope: this stage answers the INDUSTRY/STRUCTURAL question — is the pie growing,
what is being substituted, and who controls the chokepoint. The COMPANY-level
volume × price decomposition is handed to `/growth-decomposition` (06b); do not
duplicate it here.

Apply the operating-driver engine (CLAUDE.md) in its structural form. Write
`06-tech.md`:
1. **Incumbent baseline** — the solution/economics being replaced (cost/perf).
2. **New solution** — cost/perf and the crossover point where it wins on merit.
3. **Adoption curve** — rate and gating factors (cost crossover, capacity,
   qualification cycles); year-by-year migration path bear/base/bull.
4. **Components / inputs** — what it's made of; scarce or single-sourced inputs.
5. **Supply-chain chokepoints** — who holds the chokepoint and thus pricing power
   during the migration.
6. **TAM and pool** — TAM migration; express the primitive in the business's own
   units (for a transactional name, **TPV × take rate × share**, not generic
   "units × ASP") so it reconciles with the 06b decomposition.
7. **Structural implication for the subject's volume/price** — the tailwind (or
   headwind) the company-level build in 06b will sit inside.
8. **Open questions.**

Tag every number hard/soft/unsupported; be explicit where estimating.
